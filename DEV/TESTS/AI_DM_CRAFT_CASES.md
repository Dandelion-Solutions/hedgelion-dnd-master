# AI DM Craft Regression Cases

These cases adapt human GM guidance to predictable LLM runtime behavior.

## ADC01 — Player assertion inside action is not canon
Given no established secret door behind a tapestry, player says `Открываю тайную дверь за гобеленом`.
Pass: interpret the goal/action without creating the asserted door; resolve only what the PC can actually do with the established tapestry/wall.
Fail: invent the door because the sentence grammatically presupposed it.

## ADC02 — No-but preserves momentum without compensation
PC tries a method that is physically/rules-impossible.
Pass: make the blocking fact clear and expose only already-existing relevant affordances/constraints if naturally perceivable.
Fail: invent a helpful item, secret route, weaker obstacle or free alternate success just to avoid saying no.

## ADC03 — Creative method does not trigger punishment tax
Player solves a problem with an unexpected but valid method.
Pass: use the same causal/rules standard as any expected method; apply only real cost/risk.
Fail: add arbitrary complication merely because the Master had not prepared that approach.

## ADC04 — Decision-point stop
Player declares one action. Its resolution reveals a new threat/opportunity that could materially change the PC's next choice.
Pass: resolve the declared action and automatic consequences, then return control before choosing the PC's response.
Fail: autocomplete a plausible escape, attack, dialogue line or emotional reaction.

## ADC05 — Conditional declaration may continue
Player says `Обыскиваю шкаф; если там ничего важного — ухожу` and the search reveals nothing materially decision-changing.
Pass: the Master may continue through the stated condition and departure without demanding a redundant confirmation.
Fail: stop purely because every physical micro-step must be separately prompted.

## ADC06 — New information beats autocomplete
Same conditional declaration as ADC05, but the search reveals a person, danger, valuable object or major clue.
Pass: stop at the newly meaningful decision point even though the player previously said they would leave if nothing important appeared.
Fail: ignore the new information and narrate departure anyway.

## ADC07 — Worldbuilding through manifestation
PC enters a city with a distinctive law/religion/status structure.
Pass: reveal the setting primarily through concrete local behavior, environment, customs or consequences, with only the direct context needed to understand it.
Fail: lead with a long unsolicited history lecture unrelated to the immediate scene.

## ADC08 — Known context may be stated directly
The PC would obviously know a local custom or the player needs one fact for an informed choice.
Pass: state the concise relevant context plainly.
Fail: hide necessary information behind atmosphere solely to obey `show, don't tell`.

## ADC09 — Generative abundance is not world depth
One inscription mentions an old god.
Pass: establish only the history/cosmology needed now and leave remote structure undefined.
Fail: generate pantheon, wars, dynasties and factions merely because the model can.

## ADC10 — Encounters need not become combat or quests
Player spends time talking to an interesting baker/guard/scholar with no existing hook.
Pass: let the interaction remain social/ordinary if world state supports that.
Fail: automatically reveal a quest, conspiracy or attack to make the scene `matter`.

## ADC11 — One positive beat is weak preference evidence
One joke lands or one NPC receives strong attention.
Pass: treat it as weak local evidence; do not create a durable global style preference from one instance.
Fail: make future campaign content systematically comedic or NPC-centric based on that single beat.

## ADC12 — Repeated/explicit preference guides prep, not truth
Player explicitly asks for political intrigue, or repeatedly engages with politics over time.
Pass: increase preparation attention to political material when appropriate while preserving objective causality and existing canon.
Fail: retroactively make every unrelated NPC part of the political plot.

## ADC13 — Arc resolution does not spawn automatic sequel hook
Central villain/conflict of the current arc is genuinely resolved.
Pass: show actual aftermath and allow calm/downtime/epilogue; unresolved old obligations may remain if they really existed.
Fail: immediately reveal a bigger villain, secret mastermind or emergency solely to keep generation going.

## ADC14 — Arc end is not automatic campaign completion
One dungeon/boss/quest ends but the campaign's overall finality is not established.
Pass: keep campaign lifecycle active and return a natural post-resolution state to the player.
Fail: mark campaign completed just because a major encounter ended.

## ADC15 — Genuine campaign closure is allowed
An explicitly finite premise is resolved and finality is semantically clear.
Pass: allow the campaign to conclude and eventually set `completed` at an authoritative durability boundary without requiring a sequel teaser.
Fail: refuse closure by manufacturing additional plot.

## ADC16 — No authored PC epilogue choices
During an ending/epilogue, the Master knows several plausible futures for the PC.
Pass: narrate world consequences and already-established outcomes, but leave voluntary final choices/speech/emotions to the player.
Fail: choose the PC's retirement, romance, loyalty, destination or belief to make the ending neat.
