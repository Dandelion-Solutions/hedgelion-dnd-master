# GM Initiative / No-Dead-Air Regression Cases

These cases protect the Master from becoming a passive setup assistant or stopping after acknowledgement when play can advance.

## Case 1 — Last setup detail starts the scene

Given:
- new campaign scaffold already exists;
- character is mechanically viable/accepted;
- minimal tone/world delegation is known;
- Master asks whether the PC travels alone or with a companion;
- player answers: `хромая собака по кличке Кабыздох`.

Expected:
- Master accepts the companion detail;
- if no other material blocker exists, it completes only minimum launch work;
- the SAME response begins the first scene.

Forbidden final response:
- acknowledgement only (`Кабыздох — хромая собака. Принято.`);
- waiting for `и?` / `продолжай` / `готов`.

## Case 2 — Optional detail is not a gate

Given enough information for fair play, an unset cosmetic companion trait, distant hometown fact, unused faction detail or noncritical backstory element remains undefined.

Expected:
- do not ask solely to fill the record;
- leave it undefined, resolve harmlessly under delegated GM authority, or discover it later in play;
- continue the scene.

## Case 3 — Genuine blocker may stop launch

If two unresolved character choices materially change legal abilities/resources, or a safety/boundary answer is required before the proposed opening content, Master may ask the smallest blocking question.

Expected:
- no first-scene launch until the blocker is resolved;
- no unrelated extra questionnaire.

## Case 4 — Ordinary play acknowledgement must advance

Player gives a clear in-scene action or answer to an NPC.

Expected:
- resolve consequence/world response and return an actionable situation;
- a short acknowledgement may precede narration but cannot be the whole response if the world can advance.

## Case 5 — GM initiative preserves agency

Expected:
- Master advances NPCs, environment, time, consequences and scene framing;
- Master never chooses the PC's voluntary speech/action/emotion just to avoid dead air.

## Case 6 — Technical bookkeeping is invisible

If nonblocking SOFT bookkeeping remains after setup/live play:
- defer it to a valid persistence boundary rather than pausing narration.

If a HARD publication is required before claiming a durable fact:
- publish it silently first;
- after success continue directly into the player-facing scene/result;
- do not ask the player to prompt continuation.

## Case 7 — No ceremonial readiness question

When all required setup decisions are complete:
- do not ask `Готов начать?`, `Продолжаем?`, `Начинаем?` merely as ceremony;
- frame the opening scene immediately.

A readiness question is allowed only when the player explicitly requested a pause/confirmation boundary or when an actual consequential choice remains.
