## 2. Does this count as the rest a mount needs?

**Question 2: approve NO renewed boosted pace (recommended), or expressly extend the grant to this travel recovery.**

R L1089 says that after1 hour at doubled distance, mounts **need a Short or Long Rest** before repeating it. It is an actual-rest prerequisite in the travel rule, not an actually possessed feature's enumerated completion opportunity. Prayer grants benefits after a10-minute cast; it does not establish RestActivity completion.

**Recommendation:** NO. A fatigued riding horse can receive eligible HD/recharge/healing benefits but cannot immediately repeat the doubled travel hour because of Prayer alone. The alternative treats renewed pace as an additional functional rest benefit; that is plausible product interpretation, but extends the grant beyond the proposed seven feature rows. Neither choice manufactures an elapsed hour or overrides other travel limits. Feather-token bird downtime at R L19542 remains separate elapsed flying/rest time.

## Mechanical qualification — when a zero-result creature is “affected”

**Senior targeted repair:** the prior fourth-choice framing is withdrawn. Common source R L9219 says a spell's details present exactly what it does; R L9235 says that when a target cannot be affected, **nothing happens to that target**, while a slot used to cast the spell is still expended. R L13985 gates a creature that was **affected**, not merely selected. R L1470 caps restored HP and loses excess healing; zero actual HP change therefore does not by itself mean no effect. Together these clauses resolve this edge without a new PO policy. Lifecycle §8 L192 supplies the target-owned gate/reset representation; S §26 L255 requires the exact blocked-cost and linked-benefit closure, not an additional outcome choice. Current fixture `/source_mapping_lanes/0/mapping_payload/entries/98/control_transitions/0/independent_obligation` remains “Receiving benefits makes that target ineligible until its own Long Rest.”

> **Affected-target qualification and proof boundary**
>
> - A lawfully granted benefit/application or eligible opportunity counts even if its numerical delta is0 or the recipient declines optional HD/recovery. Full HP cap, already-full eligible pools and declined HD do not make an affected target eligible for repeated Prayer benefits.
> - If **all** benefit applications are actually prohibited/inapplicable, nothing happens to that target and no Prayer gate is consumed solely because it was selected or casting completed. The gate is not an extra detrimental effect that can bootstrap its own consumption when every described benefit is blocked.
> - **Unknown eligibility is not all-blocked.** Missing source/grant/health/dependency completeness remains a scoped hold; it cannot fabricate either successful application or a new unconsumed/allowed state. A proven no-effect application also cannot clear a previously consumed gate. Once consumed, only that target's own accepted Long Rest resets it.
> - No cost refund follows: R L9235 retains the slot actually used; other actual source costs remain. Each genuine new casting requires normal time/components/slot/agency and source costs. There is no automatic retry loop or free reroll. Recovery/replay of the **same** accepted attempt reuses its fixed RNG/results and cannot apply or spend twice.

**Named case: PRAYER-ZERO-ALL-BLOCKED, conditional on Q3's eventual choice.** Under Q3's recommended differentiated restriction bridge, a Simulacrum under an active Incubus or Rakshasa curse has the entire rest-benefit branch denied and direct HP healing denied by repair-only law. With complete eligibility evidence, no lawful benefit survives, so this application does not consume its gate. A replica with no otherwise-eligible nonhealth benefit presents the same edge only when that absence is proved, not assumed from missing data. If Q3 instead leaves an eligible nonhealth benefit available despite the curse, that lawful benefit/opportunity can affect the replica and consume its own gate; zero HP repair does not prevent consumption. After a curse ends, a later genuine casting still checks current actual grants and the target's existing gate; creator repair never resets it.

Consuming a target gate despite proven absence of every described effect would require an explicit new departure from these common source rules. No such departure is sought or offered as an ordinary option here. The three Q1–Q3 source-interpretation choices remain open.
