## 4. Step-6 dependency conclusions

The independent traversal established the following constraints for the adversarial review:

1. A current armed owner is not operationally recovered merely because its record is hydrated; its **complete typed future-reevaluation dependency enrollment** must also be reconstructible and bounded.
2. A DUE occurrence cannot cross into accepted execution while the same native owner still exposes that occurrence as a separate fresh candidate. Step-5.3's direct-finalization / safe-immediate-rearm / `CLAIMED(G,F)` closure remains binding.
3. For a live-owned source, the authoritative acceptance edge is the owner-native exact-source live CAS; pre-CAS local work is prospective only. No second accepted firing identity may become current because two hosts evaluated the same occurrence.
4. CORE's off-screen simulation budget is an optimization against speculative/dormant work. It cannot suppress required invalidation/recheck of an enrolled armed owner when one of its declared dependency keys materially changes.
5. `world.thread` is not semantically admitted by path existence alone. The final WP-15 result must bind the independent-lifecycle class-admission proof to the already accepted WP-11 native route and route the unreleased catalog/schema/conformance mismatch as coordinated machine alignment.
6. `THREAD_INDEX.yaml` and `CURRENT.active_threads` are discovery/summary surfaces, not proof that all independently-due temporal roots have been enumerated. Completeness-critical temporal-source routing remains a separate typed derivative contract.
7. `thread.status` lifecycle and temporal arming are related but not interchangeable. Terminal process state must not keep creating new process advancement occurrences; a `paused` label cannot silently invent a time freeze, provider rebase or deadline semantics.
8. `thread.owner_entity_id` is an in-fiction/controller association reference if retained; its field name does not make the referenced entity the HDM semantic persistence owner of the thread record.
9. Late establishment of chronology relations is supported only when admitted current evidence/anchors and protected consumer contracts make it possible. WP-15 does not promise arbitrary historical pair reconstruction after lawful compaction.
