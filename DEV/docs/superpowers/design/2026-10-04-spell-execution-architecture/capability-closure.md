## 1. Finding and recommendation

Full local execution of the 339 SRD 5.2.1 spell descriptions can reuse the accepted Activity/Rule Element/native-owner/ExecutionSegment spine. It cannot be obtained by filling 339 rows over the current admitted seed. The current owner explicitly admits eleven exact primitive contracts, ten Calculation Selectors and three Rule Operations; many necessary exact consumers and semantic variants remain absent. Names, schemas, research groups and conformance examples do not confer execution authority.

The smallest defensible extension is a finite set of shared owner-local capabilities, exact spell/mode recipes and transitive support-content admissions. Preserve one execution kernel and native state owners. Add a semantic capability only when a concrete mode proves that existing admitted semantics are insufficient. The thirteen inventory groups are discovery labels, not thirteen executors, packages or guaranteed closure units.

Two exceptional modes demonstrably require more than normal dormant admission:

1. **Wish / Roll Redo** requires an explicitly reviewed narrow reality-reconciliation amendment. Step-5.9 §2 excludes normal rewriting of established causal history; Step-3 preserves committed ancestors and immutable event meaning. A fresh ordinary roll followed by a patch to current HP is not faithful support.
2. **Time Stop** needs isolated Procedure/temporal progression and causal early termination. An ordinary local-time advance cannot lawfully progress everyone's turns or durations while claiming no time passed for other creatures.

The remaining obligations principally extend exact contracts within existing owners: spatial transition episodes; reversible form/projection and body bindings; spell-controlled Actor lifecycle; perception/knowledge/control restrictions; concentration, interruption and suppression; revival/anatomy/material commitments; and conditional long-lived occurrences. These are candidate scopes justified below, not accepted new record classes.

## 5. Decisive semantic exceptions

### 5.1 Spatial and zone episodes

Spells differ in trigger timing and cardinality. Cloudkill/Black Tentacles/Blade Barrier/Incendiary Cloud/Insect Plague/Spirit Guardians include overlapping entry, zone movement or turn events with exact limits. Spike Growth is movement-distance damage. Wall of Fire must not inherit an invented universal once-per-turn rule. Tsunami has round-linked height/damage decay and carrying; Storm of Vengeance has a numbered sequence. Prismatic Wall has seven ordered layers, layer-local destruction thresholds/counters and plane removal. Meteor Swarm/Fire Storm/Chain Lightning require compound/contiguous/anchored target geometry, not generic independent repeated targets.

Candidate minimum contract: an accepted movement or geometry transition identifies before/after material spatial basis, traversed segment when needed, exact owner episode and occurrence. The runtime discovers complete relevant intersections through bounded scene/zone indexes, then evaluates declared exposure rules. Store only counters/history that later behavior requires. A per-turn guard keys the exact spell episode + affected subject + relevant turn/round identity, according to that spell. It must distinguish zone-created-under-target, source/zone moved over target, target entering, target ending/start turn, and repeat crossing where rules distinguish them.

Creation displacement, enclosure/reaction escape, elevation/falling, force/Ethereal barriers, safe ejection and existing portals each require exact branches. A finite safety bound may block with pending mandatory work; it may not choose the first N eligible targets or silently omit distant effects. Deliberately large authored area/spawn modes require the accepted slow/cross-scope route and faithful completion, not a fast-path semantics downgrade.

### 5.4 Information, illusion, memory and agency

A minimum exact observation contract distinguishes objective state, sensory manifestation, observer recognition, fictional knowledge and human disclosure. Major Image and Programmed Illusion cannot create actual damage/conditions just because their narrative is frightening. Mirage Arcane explicitly has tactile/terrain consequences that remain when Truesight identifies the illusion. Illusion-school Creation creates material objects; school is classification, not mutation policy. Arcanist's Magic Aura changes applicable detection/targeting interpretations, not arbitrary base truth. Glibness changes a magic truth report, not actual truth. Mind Blank's Wish exception applies to protected information/observation/control; it is not total immunity to every possible Wish use.

Detect Thoughts needs language/telepathy eligibility, sense versus read/probe mode, target awareness, protected mind channel, and a private qualified response. It must not require persisting model chain-of-thought. The relevant surface thought/emotion may be a bounded material semantic proposal under source eligibility; PC voluntary thought is not silently invented by NPC cognition machinery.

Modify Memory creates an exact rules-caused memory relation with true-source/restoration provenance. Failed/interrupted casting leaves memory unchanged. Scope includes experienced-event age/length, language and description completion; remembered content does not become historical world truth. Remove Curse/Greater Restoration must restore the qualifying prior memory basis without erasing objective history or PLAYER disclosure. Step-4 and R2.2 explicitly permit genuine rules constraints, so honoring this spell is compatible with PC agency; no blanket opt-out or arbitrary voluntary loyalty authoring follows.

Suggestion/Command/Geas/Dominate modes need frozen semantic instructions and rules-specific achievable/safe/comprehensible constraints, exact action/reaction ownership, damage/completion/expiry and fallback. Dominate can command a target Reaction by spending the controller's Reaction as well; those costs belong to Procedure participants. Charmed alone does not select total control. In-fiction compelled actions do not change `world.player.controlled_pc_ids`, authenticated mutation rights or policy-adoption authority.

Divination/Commune/Contact Other Plane answers use bounded established world/subject knowledge and their exact uncertainty/unknown behavior. Future facts are not manufactured as canon to make an answer. Scrying/Locate spells need exact familiarity, distance/plane/barrier/protection and cooldown; they do not authorize loading all world records or disclosing an exact map when only a direction is supplied by the spell.

### 5.5 Counter, concentration, suppression and cost order

Counterspell's exact 2024 rule is a visible-component casting Reaction with a Constitution save. Failed save dissipates the spell and wastes its Action/Bonus Action/Reaction but does not expend the interrupted spell slot. This needs a cast commitment profile and safe parent phase; do not perform an older spell-level check or debit/refund a generic resource after committed effects.

Dispel Magic selects qualifying spell effects, uses spell origin/level and exact per-effect checks, then invokes owning teardown/support consequences. Remove Curse has different item/attunement behavior. Antimagic Field suppresses eligible ongoing magic without terminalizing it, clips areas, stops qualifying casting/Magic actions/item properties/teleportation/planar travel/portals and preserves elapsed duration. Artifact/deity origin exceptions must be inspectable. Globe of Invulnerability filters spells from outside by level; it permits targeting but excludes effect, unlike a universal target-validation veto.

Specific interaction exceptions require typed provenance/profile dispatch: Wall of Force/Forcecage resist Dispel Magic; Disintegrate destroys specified force structures; Prismatic Wall resists Antimagic and permits only its violet layer to Dispel; Daylight/Sunburst/Darkness and Moonbeam/form behavior retain exact clauses. Never solve these by deleting all Effects with a tag.

Concentration support is owner-local, not a DurationSpec mode. Admit caster support uniqueness/replacement, start/end reason, concentration saves after qualifying damage, loss on exact conditions, and descendants. Multiple simultaneous damage applications need exact source/episode and save grouping. Losing concentration, being suppressed, ending a child, caster disappearance and making a spell permanent are different transitions. True Polymorph, Glyph stored spells and Planar Binding each need their exact duration/support change; generic support DAG termination is not automatically the whole rule.

General supplied SRD rules require a separate **legal invalid-target outcome**: a valid cast on an unaffected target can still spend the slot and appear to have succeeded on a save. Binder errors/unknown references fail before RNG or mutation; rules-level non-effect must honor its expenditure and concealment. Keep diagnostic/internal explanation separate from eligible player output.

Costly materials must meet identity/quantity/value and consume-only-if-stated rules. A nonconsumed unpriced component may use qualified pouch/focus substitution with exact hand access; a costly or consumed component cannot use that shortcut. Diamonds/oils/dust/vessels/attuned forks/sigil materials are Asset bindings or a justified already-owned balance, not an invented universal gold debit. Forbiddance consumes on the final permanence-producing casting only. Astral Projection costs per target; Contingency/Glyph consume two spell casting costs at their appropriate binding stage, not at arbitrary later trigger time. Long casting interruption and completion need general spellcasting/Concentration source closure beyond this bounded extract.

### 5.6 Revival, anatomy and identity

Revivify/Raise Dead/Resurrection/True Resurrection/Reincarnate/Clone require dead-state eligibility, exact death age/cause, soul free/willing basis from the licensed general rule, body/remains eligibility, anti-revival effects, restored form/type, health result and lingering penalty/recovery. Cure Wounds/Heal/Mass Heal are not this route. Power Word Kill/Divine Word direct death does not mean generic damage-to-zero. Gentle Repose preserves specified clocks without restarting them arbitrarily. Disintegrate and soul transfer create special revival eligibility.

Body/anatomy state cannot be inferred from HP or placed in mechanical `details` with no contract. Regenerate/Resurrection/True Resurrection require exact anatomy repair/new-body creation; Reincarnate changes species traits while retaining other capabilities and prompts exact new species choices. Caster long-dead Resurrection exhaustion/incapacity and rest-based penalties need supported Condition/Procedure/temporal transitions. All components and body/identity/health/knowledge effects compose through the existing local segment; a retry cannot revive twice or consume a second diamond.

### 5.7 Conditional long-lived effects and offline dependencies

Alarm/Magic Mouth/Programmed Illusion/Glyph/Symbol/Contingency/Sequester/Imprisonment accept semantic trigger descriptions but not executable scripts. Repeated same-place castings (Arcanist's Magic Aura/Private Sanctum/Teleportation Circle/Forbiddance/Guards and Wards) need a native local streak/site/casting milestone, exact allowed gaps/location match, materials and permanence transition. Do not replay one casting 30/365 times or enumerate the whole historical log on every turn. Clone maturation, Befuddlement periodic saves, Geas daily limit and Secret Chest decay need owner-anchored sparse chronology and source-local retained counters. Hydration/process restart does not advance time. Ending spells must distinguish actual persistent output from magic that must disappear: Demiplane contents and instantaneous creation remain, generated mansion contents/familiar gear have explicit provenance/ejection/disappearance rules.
