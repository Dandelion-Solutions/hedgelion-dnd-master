### F12-01 incorporation

The accepted review found that the frozen candidate's “end turn if within 5 feet” shorthand omitted the movement condition. The integrated Command formulation says **“ends the turn if it moves within 5 feet of the caster”**, consistent with Markdown line 10229. It does not infer what happens when movement begins already inside that distance; that remains source/native reconciliation rather than a new rule.
