# S6D-09 — Domain Rules Coverage Matrix — Decision Brief

Status: **STEP 3 — HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED**

Date: 2026-08-27

## Decision

What reusable gameplay-domain surface must the built-in bounded MVP package honestly support beyond the already closed Fighter/Sorcerer exact seed?

The decision is required now because the final matrix cannot classify current CORE promises as both supported and missing. Package absence is not proof of being out of scope, and S6D-09 has no authority to reduce or expand product semantics silently.

## Established evidence

Already closed:

- exact ranged weapon attack and six exact spell/feature Activity routes;
- exact roll/attack/save/damage/healing/resource/effect primitives used by those consumers;
- HP/temp HP/LifeState/death/stable recovery;
- exact Short/Long Rest responders after a qualified boundary;
- READY_PC plus Fighter 1→2 advancement;
- eventless Mechanical-Null outcome such as an attack miss, without fake mutation.

Not closed despite current reusable CORE claims:

- generic ability/skill checks and general saves;
- initiative/turn/action/movement procedure behavior;
- visibility/reach/cover and ordinary spatial consequences;
- significant Asset equip/use/transfer;
- hazard/exploration and social uncertainty routes;
- reward/ownership transitions.

Additional currently ambiguous families include contest-specific resolution, reaction suspension without an exact seed consumer, general damage-defense content, concentration and currency economy. Their registered primitive/definition surfaces are dormant, absent or lack exact consumers.

## Options

### A — Exact seed only

Support only the existing exact character/combat/spell/health/rest/advancement routes. Narrow CORE wording so general checks, initiative-scale procedure, exploration/social mechanics and ownership changes are not built-in MVP promises.

This is smallest, but the package is closer to a conformance slice than an honestly playable general D&D MVP.

### B — Broad CORE surface

Treat every current broad CORE mechanic family as supported now and close all gaps in S6D-09.

This maximizes product coverage but expands content and primitive work well beyond the accepted bounded vertical slice.

### C — Minimal playable gameplay spine — recommended

Keep the current exact character/content slice and add only reusable routes required for ordinary open-ended play:

1. generic typed check/save;
2. initiative/turn/action/movement procedure;
3. bounded spatial inputs for supported Activities;
4. basic significant-Asset ownership/equip/use/transfer;
5. hazard and social uncertainty lowered through the generic check route;
6. reward ownership lowered through the bounded Asset-transfer route.

Remain absent/nonselectable for now: contest-specific primitive, generic reaction primitive without a real consumer, broad resistance/immunity/vulnerability content, concentration content, currency economy, crafting/downtime, teleportation, zones/entity creation and full spell/equipment/hazard corpora.

Every required primitive still needs its own Primitive Necessity Challenge. Choosing C does not activate any primitive automatically and does not authorize a generic mutation/query/workflow layer.

## Recommendation

Choose **C**.

It preserves the bounded content decision while making “playable” honest. The shared routes form a small hourglass: natural-language actions remain open-ended, GM judgment supplies only typed situational inputs, and a few deterministic consequences cover combat, exploration, social play and ownership without pre-enumerating player actions.

Strongest weakness: C is larger than the current exact seed and requires careful proof that generic check/movement/Asset consequences are reusable current needs rather than speculative abstractions.

Recommendation confidence: **MEDIUM-HIGH**.

Human decision required: **YES**.

Please choose the supported-surface boundary: **A, B or C**. If choosing C, you may amend the six listed spine items; otherwise they are the exact proposed scope.
