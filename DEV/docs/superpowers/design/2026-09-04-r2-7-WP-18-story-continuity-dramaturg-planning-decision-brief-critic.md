- `BLOCKING` — Step 1 cannot safely authorize Step 2 until repaired;
- `SIGNIFICANT` — material omission/ambiguity likely to yield wrong owner/consumer synthesis;
- `MINOR` — clarity/maintenance issue not invalidating scope.

## 3. Reconstructed preliminary BLOCKING findings

### B18-01 — Story and prospective planning collapsed into one narrative-state problem

**Severity:** `BLOCKING`

**Attack:** A generic “Story/runtime reconciliation” brief can treat both retrospective Story and future Dramaturg preparation as one narrative/memory subsystem because both are noncanonical and LLM-facing.

**Failure mode:** Story coverage accidentally selects planning currency; planning generation is mistaken for historical coverage; one generic database becomes a shadow world/recovery authority.

**Owning evidence:** Step 4; Step 5.10; R2.1; R2.5 LAW separating Story from Dramaturg horizons.

**Repair applied:** The Decision Brief now begins from the two-direction model (`past/occurred projection` versus `future/provisional preparation`), requires distinct owner/lifecycle/currentness proofs and forbids a generic memory/narrative authority.

**Disposition:** `CLOSED`.

---

### B18-02 — Existing Story architecture treated as open because legacy Story files are missing

**Severity:** `BLOCKING`

**Attack:** Missing `GAME/CORE/STORY.md`, `DEV/SPECS/story-architecture.md` and `GAME/SCHEMA/story.schema.yaml` could be interpreted as “Story design is absent.”

**Failure mode:** WP-18 reopens Step-4/5.10 semantics, recreates retired files, revives chapter ownership or invents incompatible coverage/ID/publication semantics.

**Owning evidence:** Step-4 canonical Story spec; Step-5.10 canonical durability spec; WP-11 Story physical route; verified 404s are negative machine evidence only.

**Repair applied:** The brief explicitly treats Step-4/5.10 as controlling and the missing legacy paths as stale-routing/machine-realization evidence. Recreating legacy Story architecture is out of scope.

**Disposition:** `CLOSED`.

### B18-03 — Retained planning owner presumed from catalog vocabulary or `PreparationDraft`

**Severity:** `BLOCKING`

**Attack:** Because catalog 2.0 contains planning classes and R2.4 contains `PreparationDraft`, implementation could simply persist those values.

**Failure mode:** A typed handoff/enum becomes a durable semantic owner without consumer/lifecycle proof; single-player planning becomes a correctness/recovery dependency by convenience.

**Owning evidence:** R2.4 typed-handoff law; Catalog Contracts/admission ledger; WP-10/WP-11 dormant/conditional planning disposition; R2.5 multiplayer consumer.

**Repair applied:** The brief requires owner admission per retained planning surface, preserves no durable single-player owner by default, and forbids serialization itself from creating authority.

**Disposition:** `CLOSED`.

---

### B18-04 — Shared Dramaturg horizon underframed across authorization/currentness/privacy

**Severity:** `BLOCKING`

**Attack:** “Store one shared planning document” ignores independent participant sessions, private player-local planning, current PLAYER/control bindings, campaign/LIVE movement and concurrent planning edits.

**Failure mode:** private information leaks; stale writer overwrites current plan; LWW/transport order selects planning; planning becomes LIVE/current truth; mode transitions reuse stale plan unvalidated.

**Owning evidence:** R2.5 two-level Dramaturg coordination; WP-16 authorization/currentness; WP-17 recipient-safe projection; R2.3 eligibility.

**Repair applied:** The brief makes writer eligibility, recipient visibility, generation/CAS/rebase, invalidation, mode transition, recovery and bounded discovery mandatory Step-2 questions.

**Disposition:** `CLOSED`.

**Severity:** `BLOCKING`

**Attack:** A prose-only reconciliation can map canonical requirements to files but fail to reverse-audit convenient machine fields, or inspect machine surfaces without proving every accepted semantic obligation has a realization.

**Repair applied:** The brief now requires explicit architecture->machine and machine->architecture proof, including owner classification for every relevant field/root/value/control/index/test surface.

**Disposition:** `CLOSED`.
