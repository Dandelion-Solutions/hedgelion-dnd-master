# R2.7 WP-09 Step 5 — Candidate Context-Runtime Realization Mapping

Status: **CANDIDATE — ADVERSARIAL REVIEW REQUIRED**

## Purpose

This candidate turns the accepted R2.3/R2.4/R2.6 logical law into a narrow,
implementation-facing allocation. It does not select files, physical topology,
schema/catalog changes, provider facilities, a cache product or an implementation
plan. Existing semantic owners remain primary.

## Candidate laws

### C-WP09-1 — Separate engine instruction cache from role context

The complete exact-package CORE instruction cache remains immutable in-chat
operational support. It is neither campaign canon nor a RoleContextBundle.
A role-context request may use already-present engine instructions, but its
campaign packet is assembled only from the registered profile, admitted sources
and bounded R2.3 route. Activating an already cached module cannot authorize
campaign-world preload.

### C-WP09-2 — Treat compact records exclusively as routing/discovery support

CURRENT, active scenes, scene/location/entity indexes, explicit references,
live/current routes and bounded history hints may seed discovery before body load.
Each material candidate is resolved through its routed current native owner and
role eligibility before semantic use. Index/current/scene omission does not prove
absence; cache presence does not prove currentness; an internal eligibility read
does not itself become role evidence.

### C-WP09-3 — Keep packet control runtime-local and profile-owned

A registered consumer's ContextNeedProfile owns purpose, legal channels, typed
finite expansion, required versus optional classification, representation floors,
finite limits and registered fallback. The Context Runtime is runtime-local and
ephemeral: it yields RoleContextBundle, ContextTrace and context-source basis
without durable control records. It may consume compact routing support and
existing native owners, but it creates no generic memory/vector/graph service,
background worker or persistent fairness ledger.

### C-WP09-4 — Apply one conservative allocation path

The runtime first assembles all required members at legal floors. Only then may it
rank already legal optional/supporting candidates. One central conservative
estimator is the resource control point; it must not assume exact remaining
provider token/context telemetry or use a fixed provider percentage. If required
floors cannot be satisfied, it returns terminal `UNSATISFIABLE`; it neither
silently drops a required dependency nor loops/reprofiles/guesses. The registered
caller selects one finite R2.4 alternative.

### C-WP09-5 — Preserve finite history and role boundaries

History escalation identifies an unresolved typed dependency, source family and
finite bound. Broad continuity/history can orient a request but cannot preload
deep history or replace material source escalation. Phase rebind uses the
registered profile/bundle/basis and transports only the minimum typed result.
MechanicalContext, deterministic continuation, resolution trace, another
role-bundle's physical presence and DEV/package/CI surfaces do not confer
role-context source eligibility or role-private continuity/control.

### C-WP09-6 — Treat verification as behavior, not vocabulary

Acceptance must prove bounded registered discovery, routed currentness/eligibility,
floor preservation, lawful degradation, terminal finite fallback and no hidden
token dependency. It must retain ordinary-turn negative proof against broad
campaign/history scans and prove cache/index/trace/MechanicalContext authority
separation. Existing catalog registrations and lazy-read tests are supporting
evidence, never sole conformance proof.

## Candidate acceptance probes

1. A cached CORE module changes semantic activation but does not add campaign
   reads.
2. A relevant entity omitted from a scene/index remains discoverable through an
   admitted alternate route; a stale/index-only body is not semantically used.
3. A required exact/structured floor remains intact while optional material is
   reduced; an infeasible required set terminates with a registered caller path.
4. The same profile result does not rely on exact remaining host-token telemetry.
5. An ordinary turn neither scans campaign directories/history nor reuses a
   MechanicalContext/trace/continuation as role context.
6. A history need escalates to its typed finite owner route rather than scanning
   the whole log.
7. A re-bound phase receives only legal typed transport and cannot inherit
   another role's private source eligibility.
