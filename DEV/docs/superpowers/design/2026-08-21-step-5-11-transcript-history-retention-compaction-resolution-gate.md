# 2. Resolved adversarial blockers

## R1 — semantic-content discharge

Resolved law:

> Exact payload may be compacted only when every surviving semantic consumer either needs no content beyond occurrence/provenance identity or independently retains the proposition/content required by its contract.

A `message_id` alone cannot stand in for lost semantic meaning.

## R2 — minimal exact representation

Resolved law:

> Exact protection is scoped to the smallest sufficient accepted-text representation: whole payload only when necessary; otherwise exact slice or canonical-owner copy.

This prevents one exact password/quote from permanently retaining unrelated OOC/private text in the same host message.

## R3 — Transcript exact-certification

Resolved law:

> Story exactness is deterministic and content-specific. A retained Transcript record supports a verbatim claim only while its exact content/slice digest still matches surviving source verification evidence.

Material editorial edit may keep the Story record but revokes verified-exact status if it no longer matches.

## R4 — source candidate monotonicity

Resolved law:

> Step-5.10 Transcript candidate identity is the stable message envelope/source position, not the presence of exact payload.

Payload compaction changes availability state, not source identity. `MAY_OMIT` may later terminate without output; `MUST_MATERIALIZE` blocks compaction.

## R5 — live-safe message identity

Resolved law:

> Message evidence born under a live epoch uses Step-5.8-compatible collision-free source-native identity and retains it through close/absorption. Independent source enumeration domains are not implicitly comparable.

Legacy campaign-sequential `runtime.message` catalog policy is machine-realization debt where it conflicts.

## R6 — Step-3 raw linkage clarification

Resolved interpretation:

> `runtime.interaction` retains stable linkage to accepted message evidence identity. Step 3 does not require eternal exact payload after Step-5.11 lawful compaction.

Any exact mechanics dependency remains separately protected.

## R7 — outbound qualification

Resolved handoff:

> Generated Narrator prose is not yet durable evidence that text was emitted/exposed. Step 5.12 supplies the qualifying host-delivery boundary before outbound `runtime.message`/Transcript occurrence semantics may be established.

## R8 — protection-routing durability

Resolved law:

> Correctness-relevant exact dependency enrollment/release and sufficient bounded protection routing participate in the required durable closure before compaction may rely on their absence/presence.

A stale derived reverse index cannot authorize irreversible payload loss.
