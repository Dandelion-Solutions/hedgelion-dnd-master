## WR-01. Entry, eligibility and semantic boundary

Only an admitted Wish Roll Redo Activity mode can initiate this profile. It selects one actual accepted die-roll occurrence made within the last round, including the caster's last turn. The eligibility predicate uses the exact Procedure/BoundaryOccurrence/chronology bridge applicable to the original roll and Wish initiation. Git time, wall time, message ordinal, a global round counter or a guessed six-second interval are not interchangeable evidence. When no valid comparison is established, retain a typed input/adjudication/currentness hold before acceptance; never manufacture eligibility.

The caster may choose normal, Advantage or Disadvantage for the new roll and, after seeing it, choose the original or new roll. Preserve all original raw draws and selected result. New draws have new request/generation identity owned by this Wish Resolution and use only authoritative op.roll. No ordinary retry permission is broadened. Choosing the original still counts as this nonduplication Wish mode and retains its casting costs/stress. Every Wish stress consequence, including unpreventable damage, Strength/recovery and permanent casting-loss chance, remains under the spell's exact ordinary cost/effect profile.

There is one current reality and immutable evidence of the prior accepted reality. The new relation does not edit old Event payloads, roll identities, accepted player choices, messages or physical exposure. It does not create a cycle in CAUSES. The new Wish cause is forward of its target evidence and establishes replacement-validity for current consequences, not backward causal ancestry.
