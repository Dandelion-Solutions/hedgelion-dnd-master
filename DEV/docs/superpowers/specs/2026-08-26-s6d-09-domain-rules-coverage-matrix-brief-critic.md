The initial brief required family coverage but did not prove that every identity-bound package consumer/dependency, every active machine consumer edge and every explicit product promise entered the ledger. Thematic family sampling could therefore miss an orphan or overclaim completeness.

Required repair: define exact typed source sets, their finite union, ledger equality, reverse provenance, consumer-edge closure and zero-difference exit evidence.

### S2 — Package absence was conflated with product scope

The initial disposition vocabulary could classify a product-promised but package-absent mechanic as out of the MVP. Missing realization is not affirmative scope evidence.

Required repair: separate package presence, product scope and realization; introduce `SUPPORTED_GAP`; require current product-owner evidence for `OUT_OF_SUPPORTED_MVP_SEED`.

### S3 — Atomic route rows lacked cross-cutting execution evidence

Rows did not require exact ruleset/profile applicability, typed failures, binding/freeze/currentness, retry/conflict/revision identity or multiplayer behavior. Several high-risk families were only implicit.

Required repair: add those fields with explicit N/A reasons and explicitly census initiative/turn order/action budgets, advantage/proficiency, visibility/cover/senses, damage defenses, concentration/support and ownership transfer.

Matrix-local RED→GREEN evidence could be misread as sufficient to change or activate a completed S6D-01…08 owner.

Required repair: require the affected owner’s explicit regression/amendment gate, RED→GREEN evidence and renewed independent whole-project review; forbid matrix-local activation or supersession.
