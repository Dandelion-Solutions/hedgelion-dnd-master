## Decision

The owner selects:

> **B — REUSE-FIRST STORY/HISTORY CONTINUITY PROJECTIONS**

R2.1 SHALL NOT introduce a generic `memory` or `continuity` semantic owner.

Long-campaign continuity is modeled as a source/lifecycle taxonomy over existing HDM owners and derived projections:

```text
CURRENT AUTHORITATIVE SOURCES
    natural world/runtime/knowledge/disclosure owners

ACCEPTED HISTORICAL EVIDENCE
    Interaction / runtime.message / SemanticEvent /
    MechanicalEvent / chronology / other admitted history

STORY
    durable noncanonical source-bound history projection
    may serve eligible gameplay roles as broad/episodic orientation

TRANSIENT WORKING CONTINUITY
    current working set / generated recap
    disposable and non-authoritative

SELECTIVE EXACT EVIDENCE
    Step-5.11 protected exact sources / natural exact owner /
    verified exact Transcript where applicable

OPTIONAL FUTURE DERIVED VIEWS
    admitted only after a concrete downstream consumer proves need
```

---

## Approved R2.1 direction

The owner approves the Decision Brief direction R2.1-L1 through R2.1-L12 for mechanical formalization:

1. continuity is not a new authority;
2. current owners answer current questions;
3. eligible Story may serve as derived gameplay continuity;
4. derived continuity does not widen role/player/subject eligibility;
5. correctness-critical claims return to the proper source class;
6. durable derived alignment uses HDM source evidence, not host-chat ancestry;
7. only admitted HDM evidence may enter durable continuity;
8. stale/incompatible projection degrades to stronger evidence rather than guessing;
9. derived text cannot self-amplify into stronger factual authority;
10. per-entity continuity begins as a scoped view, not a new durable synopsis;
11. exact recall remains the Step-5.11 Selective Exact contract;
12. no per-turn/background continuity-maintenance correctness dependency is introduced.

---

## Product semantics preserved

This decision does not change:

- natural current-state ownership;
- `world.knowledge` fictional epistemic authority;
- `runtime.disclosure` human exposure authority;
- Story nonauthority;
- Step-5.11 semantic-continuity/selective-exact product promise;
- host Retry/Edit/branch/delete non-rewind semantics;
- recovery from HDM-owned evidence rather than hidden model/context memory;
- the requirement that role/player/subject eligibility be evaluated independently of physical/storage availability.

Story gains an additional **consumer role** as eligible gameplay continuity/orientation input. It does not gain new semantic authority.

---

## Rejected / deferred by this decision

Rejected for current R2.1 scope:

- dedicated generic gameplay-memory subsystem;
- host-history age/stability timer;
- generic ChatGPT-history ancestry tree;
- global scalar source-trust score;
- universal exact transcript promise;
- background summarization worker as correctness requirement.

Conditional:

- durable per-entity synopsis — revisit only if R2.3 demonstrates that bounded entity-scoped retrieval over existing owners/history/Story cannot satisfy latency/context/quality needs;
- additional derived continuity products — require a concrete downstream consumer and normal architecture admission.

Not adopted as a generic R2.1 rule:

- one durable mutation per summary assessment. A bounded source-traceable derived publication may legitimately contain several coherent projection records. R2.2 may reconsider a narrower one-Actor mutation rule if cognition semantics justify it.

## Stage boundaries

R2.1 owns:

- continuity source classes;
- authority/lifecycle semantics;
- source/provenance/coverage requirements;
- stability/consolidation semantics;
- history alignment;
- stale/repair/rebuild/retirement behavior;
- semantic versus exact recall promise;
- Story/Chronicler continuity relationship;
- generative derived-projection validation boundaries.

R2.1 does **not** decide:

- Actor cognition/goals/relationships — R2.2;
- ranking/token budgets/placement/retrieval algorithm — R2.3;
- single-context role execution/instruction composition — R2.4;
- multiplayer coordination/catch-up protocol — R2.5;
- final host assurance — R2.6;
- schemas/catalogs/runtime implementation — R2.7 mapping and later implementation planning.
