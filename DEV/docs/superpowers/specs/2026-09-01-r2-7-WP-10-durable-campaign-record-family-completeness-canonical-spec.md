Status: **CANONICALIZATION CHECKPOINT — PENDING MANDATORY SENIOR AUDIT**

## Canonical allocation

WP-10 adopts Alternative A as an implementation-facing *documentation-level
logical allocation matrix*. It is not a registry, service, subsystem or new
semantic owner.

1. Actor private continuity and directed relationships remain source-Actor-local.
2. `world.knowledge` is one subject/fact relation; legacy fields are never parallel authority.
3. Effect/application is natural-owner-local. TemporalBinding remains with its native temporal owner; Agenda is derived.
4. Runtime lifecycle is a namespace with separate Interaction, IntentPlan, Command, Procedure, Resolution and Continuation members. Runtime evidence separately retains MechanicalEvent, Receipt and ResolutionTrace.
5. History/delivery is a namespace with separate SemanticEvent/relation, Disclosure and retained Message members. Their mutation and retention contracts do not merge.
6. Story content/progress is a noncanonical projection with its retained Story-layer source basis only. R2.3/WP-09 runtime source basis is ephemeral and has no campaign record.
7. ID allocator is campaign operational. Collaboration is conditional; Dramaturg horizons are conditional and multiplayer-only projections.

## Boundaries

This allocation selects no physical path, schema, encoding, HOT representation,
topology, migration, template/generator or bootstrap behavior. It authorizes no
runtime, schema, catalog, CORE, test or implementation-planning change.
