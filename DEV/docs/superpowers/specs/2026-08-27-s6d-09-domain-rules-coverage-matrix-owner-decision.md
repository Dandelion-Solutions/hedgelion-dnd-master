# S6D-09 — Supported Surface Owner Decision

Status: **HUMAN OWNER DECISION — ACCEPTED**

Date: 2026-08-27

The built-in MVP adopts **C — Minimal Playable Gameplay Spine**. It honestly supports ordinary open-ended adventure play without promising a broad D&D corpus.

The supported additions are generic typed check/save; minimal initiative/turn/action/movement Procedure; bounded engine-owned spatial inputs; basic significant Asset ownership/equip/use/transfer; hazard and social uncertainty lowered through check/save; reward ownership lowered through the same Asset transition; and the already accepted exact S6D-07/08 routes.

This decision does not enumerate player actions, create a universal rules DSL, tactical map/pathfinder, inventory/economy engine, hazard/social/reward engines, or grant primitive activation authority. A new primitive still requires an exact consumer and Primitive Necessity Challenge; compiler lowering, existing owner transition and GM adjudication plus typed consequence remain preferred.

Contest-specific resolution, generic reaction, broad damage-defense and concentration content, currency economy, crafting/downtime, teleportation, zones/entity creation, and broad equipment/spell/hazard corpora remain absent/nonselectable until a concrete supported consumer proves necessity.

The bounded spatial item does not promise generic visibility/cover calculation or activate adjudicated visibility/reachability facts. Hazard lowering does not promise a generic hazard time/resource engine; an exact existing time/resource owner may be used only when an independently admitted supported consequence requires it.

Step 4 must prove one end-to-end architecture walkthrough across social uncertainty, exploration/hazard, movement/spatial judgment, initiative/combat and reward Asset transfer, reusing this small spine rather than action-specific primitives or domain engines.
