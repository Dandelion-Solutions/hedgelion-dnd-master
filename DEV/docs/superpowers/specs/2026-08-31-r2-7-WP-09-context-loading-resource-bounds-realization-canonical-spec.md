# R2.7 WP-09 — Context Loading, Retrieval and Resource-Bounded Operation — Realization Canonical Specification

Status: **CANONICALIZATION CHECKPOINT — PENDING MANDATORY SENIOR AUDIT**

## 1. Authority and scope

This specification is the narrow WP-09 realization allocation for the accepted
R2.3 Context Runtime, R2.4 single-context execution, R2.6 host assurance and
closed WP-08 role-context realization. Those primary specifications continue to
own their semantic laws. This document fixes how their context/resource contract
must relate to current runtime support surfaces.

It creates no runtime/schema/catalog/CORE change, physical module choice,
durable record root, index partition, HOT/SQLite mechanism, provider capability,
numeric target or implementation plan.

## 2. Normative realization laws

### LAW WP09-1 — Engine cache and campaign packet are distinct

The exact-package full CORE instruction cache is immutable in-chat operational
support. It is not campaign canon, a role-context source basis, a
RoleContextBundle, or ChatGPT Memory. A registered profile's campaign packet is
assembled separately through the bounded context route. Semantic activation of an
already cached CORE module does not authorize preload or semantic use of
unrelated campaign/world/history data.

### LAW WP09-2 — Compact discovery does not confer authority

CURRENT, active scene, scene/location/entity INDEX, explicit reference,
live/current route and bounded history hint may seed candidate discovery before
full body load. They remain routing support. Before material semantic use, a
candidate must resolve its routed current native owner and role eligibility.
Index/scene/current omission is not semantic absence; cache presence is not
currentness; a minimal internal eligibility read is not automatically admissible
role evidence.

### LAW WP09-3 — Packet control is registered, runtime-local and ephemeral

The registered consumer's ContextNeedProfile owns purpose, channels, typed finite
expansion, required/optional classification, legal representation floors, finite
limits and fallback registration. The Context Runtime assembles its bounded
RoleContextBundle, ContextTrace and ephemeral source basis at runtime. These
control artifacts have no durable WP-09 representation and create no generic
memory, vector/graph service, background worker or persistent fairness ledger.
They may use current native owners and derived routing support without replacing
either's authority.

### LAW WP09-4 — Allocation is conservative and terminal

All required members are assembled first at their legal floors; only remaining
capacity may serve legal optional/supporting candidates. One central conservative
estimator controls allocation and may not depend on exact remaining host
token/context telemetry or a fixed provider percentage. If required floors cannot
be met, assembly returns terminal `UNSATISFIABLE`. For that failed assembly
attempt, the registered caller selects exactly one finite R2.4 alternative and
terminates the attempt. It must not chain alternatives, loop, guess, silently
drop a required dependency, invent a profile or reprofile.

### LAW WP09-5 — Continuity, phase and mechanical boundaries remain typed

Broad continuity/history can orient an eligible request but cannot replace
material source escalation or justify preload. History escalation names an
unresolved typed dependency, source family and finite bound. Each phase rebinds
its registered profile/bundle/source basis and receives only minimum legal typed
transport. MechanicalContext, deterministic continuation, resolution trace,
another role bundle's physical presence, DEV catalog/package and CI surfaces do
not confer role-context eligibility, source authority or role-private
continuity/control.

### LAW WP09-6 — Conformance is behavioral

Catalog registrations, schemas and existing lazy-read tests are supporting
surfaces, not sole proof. Future approved implementation/release work must prove:
bounded registered discovery; routed currentness/eligibility; legal required
floors before optional reduction; lawful degraded result; terminal one-alternative
fallback; no exact hidden-token dependency; no ordinary campaign/history scan;
and cache/index/trace/MechanicalContext authority separation.
