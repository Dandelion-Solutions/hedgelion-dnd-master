## 1. Product result and boundary

The target is faithful local support for all 339 SRD 5.2.1 spell entries, including their required modes, exceptions, costs, durations, interactions and recovery obligations. The player describes intent; mathematical rules remain complete even when their presentation is hidden. Ordinary GAME execution does not fetch spell rules or ask a separate model to calculate them.

This target extends the bounded six-spell package. It is not a claim that all classes, levels, feats, items or creature abilities are already implemented. Class acquisition and READY_PC selection must independently admit the spells they offer. Runtime capacity and missing campaign facts are distinct from missing rule support. A release claiming full support may not offer a supposedly supported cast and discover a missing capability after spending its resources.

The existing Activity, MechanicalContext, native Actor/Asset/Effect/Zone/Procedure/Information, HOT, execution, temporal, publication and recovery owners are reused. The 13 research groups organize content analysis; they do not become 13 services, executors or LLM roles. No new physical model topology, online rule service, general scripting language, coordinate-world simulator or second canonical state store is proposed.

## 3. Offline content and the support unit — SP-01

A supported unit is one spell plus every required mode and its transitive rule/content dependencies. Its definition supplies exact Activity entry points, parameters, casting profile, target contracts, scaling, costs, effects, triggers, termination and presentation references through existing catalog kinds. A derived support inventory maps each source requirement to an exact consumer and proof. It owns no gameplay state or package identity.

Required dependencies include applicable conditions, common casting rules, senses/vision/cover predicates, damage-defense policy, action economy, required component/asset profiles, rest/chronology rules, and eligible stat blocks and actions referenced by forms or summons. References such as any eligible Beast are closed by the chosen legal package domain and complete eligible-set proof, not by handpicking the familiar's example list. Spell-created stat blocks are provisioned with the spell. Full SRD spell support includes the eligible SRD form/summon/support content that those spells require; content outside the adopted lawful corpus is not silently invented or promised.

Classes, items and external books remain separately scoped except where an actual spell mode directly needs an independently closed dependency. In particular Wish feat replacement requires a complete eligible SRD feat dependency domain and prerequisite graph, not arbitrary feat names; polymorphic monster actions need their real mechanics rather than a decorative stat block. Wish's open GM mode remains adjudication, not an implicit promise to implement every game system or external book.

Runtime package content is locally available and immutable for an adopted rules context. Build-time verification traverses its explicit dependency closure; normal casting does not. Packaging follows the existing manifest -> package snapshot -> exact resolved lock -> generation-qualified set identity chain. Runtime reads no DEV files. Cards, indexes and compiled representations are derived from this chain and cannot become a competing digest/admission owner. Required attribution and independent public formulation apply.

## 5. Build, load and cache — SP-03

Cold initialization validates the adopted lock and compiled artifact once per coherent immutable identity. Package adoption/invalidation recomputes it; ordinary warm casting does not rescan package members or recompile recipes.

The compiled cache key includes generation-qualified ruleset set identity, catalog-context fingerprint generation/value, Activity identity and semantic generation/hash, engine-contract/compiler generation and adopted mode/policy profile where compilation depends on it. A changed identity cannot reuse an object accidentally. Compiler layout/cache format never becomes gameplay state. A stale/corrupt derived object is discarded and rebuilt from the exact local adopted inputs; unavailable authoritative content blocks before acceptance rather than searching the internet.

MechanicalContext caching additionally keys the committed/prospective state view, explicit entity/subject bindings, relevant owner revisions, source projections, exact accepted fact fingerprint and materially applied policy refs. A disposable derived value is not trusted across suspension without the Step-3 safe recompute/re-pin rules. Accepted facts, RNG and committed outcomes retain their own causal identities.

## 6. Intent lookup and prompt size — SP-04

Known spell identity uses local direct lookup. Natural-language mapping starts from the Actor's actually granted/known/prepared eligible capability index, localized aliases and concise effect descriptors. Detailed cards load only for plausible current candidates and the selected spell/mode. A broader rules question or creative request can use a separate local catalog lookup without granting its results or importing hidden NPC mechanics into player context.

The entire corpus and every supporting stat block never enter the ordinary prompt. Selection narrows from cheap metadata before optional content hydration and Context budget allocation. Use existing Context source eligibility, novelty and coverage laws; do not invent a second retrieval agent. Ambiguity is resolved internally when equivalent, or by one small question only when cost/risk/consequence differs materially. Exact player vocabulary is not a gate.

## 7. Cast preflight and costs — SP-05

One bound cast profile validates current availability, source and spell level, selected slot/ability, current action/reaction entitlement, components and free hands/focus rules, material price/consumption, targeting, accepted spatial facts and any exact casting exclusions. Engine-owned eligibility comes from MechanicalContext/native state. The casting origin may be an exact admitted familiar relay or item/source profile and is not necessarily the caster's location.

Resource and Asset costs use exact reservation/commit points. Casting-action expenditure, slot expenditure, consumed component expenditure and eventual effect creation are separate rule-defined facts. Long casting/ritual work owns its exact start, repeated action/concentration, interruption and completion boundaries; elapsed fiction uses chronology, never wall-clock elapsed chat time. Ritual does not automatically mean free or instantaneous casting. Upcasting is a registered bounded definition expression, not model arithmetic or copied values.

The current common casting profile enforces one spell slot expended to cast a spell per turn, with exact admitted exceptions; it does not import an older universal one-leveled-spell rule. Slotless item/feature/ritual casts retain their actual action and eligibility rules. A prepared ritual takes ten minutes longer and does not spend a slot. A cast taking at least one minute requires the Magic action each turn and concentration; loss of concentration interrupts that casting without spending its slot. Armor-training eligibility, verbal/somatic/material access, spell save DC and spell attack modifiers are exact current common consumers. A spell-specific or feature exception must be explicitly bound rather than inferred from a name or generic casting mode.

Reaction/counter failure must preserve the particular spell's cost semantics. For example the current-edition Counterspell mode spends the interrupted casting action while not expending its slot; it is not implemented through a universal refund. More generally no blanket refund or compensation policy is inferred from a failed spell.

Malformed binding, missing native reference and unsupported package are pre-acceptance faults. A mechanically valid cast directed at an invalid creature/object type is a different rule outcome: preserve the published slot/action/component expenditure and no-effect semantics, and do not reveal a hidden invalidity by a binder error or diagnostic. Apparent save-success presentation may be required. Reusable target preflight cannot turn this rule into a free test of hidden creature type. Likewise the cast range is not automatically an ongoing effect leash: continued range/distance limits apply only where the spell declares them. Common material substitution is restricted to the exact nonconsumed, unpriced component/focus/pouch and hand contract.

## 8. One local execution until a lawful exit — SP-06

The existing host composes binding, preflight, closed primitive dispatch, Calculation, authoritative RNG, prospective native owner changes and segment acceptance. It processes the current IntentPlan until completion, typed failure, genuine choice/reaction/adjudication or currentness/capacity suspension. Deterministic substeps do not invoke the LLM. One local orchestration is the normal sufficient-input path, not a universal one-commit promise across LIVE owners.

Each primitive reads declared exact roles/accessors and exports a typed result. Mutation plans are prospective, parent-relative and validated by the owning native profile. A model cannot supply a trusted event payload or assert that an arbitrary mutation happened. Segment acceptance establishes all coupled native owner facts atomically with their revisions, fixed RNG, event identities and receipt basis. Existing HOT admission/currentness, access, durability and LIVE publication fences remain mandatory.

Multiple intent clauses and already committed segments retain partial completion when a later clause fails. Coupled spell consequences are split only at a rule- and owner-lawful edge. No generic rollback, loop, callback, property path, arbitrary Signal/StateDelta envelope or script is admitted. An exact new typed variant is introduced only for a proved consumer and its complete contract.

## 9. Determinism and recovery — SP-07

Fix all material pre-roll rules, accepted fictional inputs and authoritative engine state before observing randomness. Roll identity and raw values belong to accepted causal evidence and are reused for retry. The recipe may generate later dependent rolls only after their required branches are known; independent known rolls may be batched locally when rule order remains unchanged.

Continuation retains the exact Activity/context identity, execution position, accepted bindings/facts/policy basis, generated RNG, typed exports, affected revisions and pending offer/generation identities. It retains neither a trusted prospective delta nor an open database transaction. Revalidation uses Step-3 frontiers. An incompatible adopted package/state fails with an existing typed currentness/context failure; no silent rebinding, fresh die or vanished cost follows.

Cold/load/save/LIVE/recovery restore native owners and protected accepted execution evidence, then rebuild compiled caches, Effect aggregates, relation indexes and Agenda. Presentation or publication failure cannot re-execute an accepted spell or allocate replacement stable IDs.

## 10. Effects, concentration, repeated work and suppression — SP-08

One `world.effect` is one target-local application with source/rules origin, exact declared parameters, application order, support parent, lifecycle and native temporal/trigger state. Spell-specific matching/arbitration/stacking rules decide replacement, refresh, coexistence and suppression. Condition presence/value remains derived, not an Actor list. Required valued-condition activation closes `condition.value` and exact aggregation before use.

Concentration uses the already designed support graph: an exact caster-local concentration support Effect is the parent of dependent target/zone applications. Starting a replacement concentration, its loss or caster incapacity computes the complete scoped support closure and atomically terminates the required dependents. Damage-driven concentration saves use actual accepted damage and current save policy, fixed rolls and exact occurrence identity. Required damage-instance grouping and all caster exceptions are owned by that admitted profile, not guessed from receipt text. No second mutable Actor concentration flag is introduced.

Per-turn, delayed, repeated-save and long-lived spell work uses owner-local trigger/TemporalBinding occurrences, indexed by exact accepted boundary/chronology dependencies. Once-per-turn/round restrictions retain their owning occurrence/generation and causal evidence, not an untyped timestamp. Ending a spell removes armed work atomically and rebuildable indexes follow native state. Suppressed applications keep their identity and deadlines; suppression is not termination or a pause of duration unless an exact rule says so.

The current common rule's caster dismissal of an ongoing time-span spell without an action is a distinct exact consumer: validate non-Incapacitated caster, source episode and permitted termination scope, then apply full atomic support teardown. Do not charge a generic remove-effect action, dismiss unrelated sources or extend this classification to instantaneous/permanent creations whose own mode differs.

## 11. Space, zones and movement — SP-09

`Actor.location_id` remains a durable location, not micro-position. Target/Area declarations constrain a finite complete candidate set; they do not discover the world. Existing invocation facts are admitted per exact consumer and binding. Visibility, line/cover, occupied space, enclosure and zone crossing require exact additional typed fact/profile contracts; a generic string/object of geometry assertions is forbidden. A complete scene roster/boundary source proves affected-set completeness before RNG/commit. If fiction is insufficient, ask for the missing circumstance before rolling.

Within-location spatial relations may remain fiction with frozen invocation judgment. A persistent Zone owns its admitted geometry/placement, participation episode and relationship to source/support under a closed profile; its derived spatial index never owns truth. Movement declares start/end/crossing facts for every affected admitted zone edge, enabling entry/start/end/exit triggers and per-occurrence re-entry semantics. This is a typed scene-local relation contract, not a mandatory grid, pathfinder or physics engine.

Shape predicates are source-specific. Fire Storm's requirement that each cube be adjacent to at least one other cube does not imply that all cubes form one connected component. Wall of Ice's neighbor requirement and Guards and Wards' contiguous protected area retain their different predicates. Validate the exact finite placement relation before acceptance; no generic connected-area rule may narrow legal shapes. Native location/connection/Asset relations also preserve extra-dimensional contents and identities after an access portal ends when the actual spell permits this. A closed portal does not imply deletion of its destination, inhabitants or contents.

Teleportation/planar relocation validates destination ownership and eligibility, party/mount/asset consequences, chronology provider compatibility and required LIVE transfer boundaries. It never substitutes `procedure.spend_movement` for a durable move or treats an unknown destination as a guessed canonical place. Falling and forced relocation have exact causal consequence profiles when a spell requires them.

## 12. Forms, entities, gear and identity — SP-10

A temporary form references an eligible local archetype/stat block and a spell-specific retained/replaced/granted field policy. Current derived statistics and capability eligibility are projected from the same Actor and supporting Effects; the original build/Actor identity is not overwritten with a second character sheet. Mutable HP/resources remain under their native owner. Equipment stays canonical Asset state with exact merge/suppression/drop/resize obligations; effects ending restore eligibility, not stale copied snapshots.

Current-edition Polymorph retains Actor HP and supplies form-derived temporary HP; it must not import an older replacement-HP model. Its anatomy/casting/gear restrictions and temporary-HP termination differ from Shapechange and other form modes. Every form profile closes overlapping forms, form switch, suppression, dispel, zero-HP/LifeState, retained abilities/actions and restoration rules. Any HP provenance/conditional cleanup member needed by a specific profile requires a typed Actor/Health amendment, not an Effect parameter pretending to own HP.

Summons use the existing Actor kind, native identity allocation and eligible stat blocks/actions. Creation, initiative/procedure insertion, commands/default actions, sensory/touch relay, linked benefits, control expiry, dismissal/death, equipment drop and recovery are explicit mode contracts. They do not create an LLM per creature or precompute private minds. Command control and creature identity are distinct; control expiry need not delete a permanent creature.

Unseen Servant uses the same native Actor as a sparse stat-bearing magical-force proxy under an explicit Actor/archetype profile, with no private mind or automatically granted creature type. The profile closes HP/zero-HP disappearance (not character death saves), defense/strength, location, command/task/action restrictions, exact distance ending and source lifetime. Its use of an Actor carrier never establishes creature-only spell eligibility: each exact target-domain predicate comes from the admitted force profile and common/source rule reconciliation. Any source-ambiguous target classification is an explicit bounded adjudication/policy input at that exact consumer, fixed before outcome, rather than guessed HP/type or an unconditional class grant. This selected carrier introduces no new world-record kind or LLM.

Creature/object conversion, linked body/soul/proxy modes and permanent change have exact Actor/Asset/Effect/Location profiles with atomic creation/placement/health and stable identity rules. Magic Jar, Clone, Astral Projection, Animate Objects and True Polymorph cannot be replaced by a generic `form_id` or arbitrary entity patch. Linked-body state requires a closed owner amendment; no universal soul service or generic graph follows.

### 12.1 Chosen body/principal factoring — SP-22

The chosen extension uses native Actors for each independently damageable corporeal/astral representation, the existing stable Actor identity for the principal, native Assets for containers/components and a typed relation embedded in a spell-root Effect. There is no `world.soul` or general identity graph. In ordinary embodiment the principal and physical Actor are the same record; split only when an actual admitted spell requires independent bodies. A newly allocated body/proxy representation has one native HP/LifeState/location authority and no copied private mind or independently chosen build. Its exact immutable construction/retained-stat projection basis references the principal or admitted archetype; current mutable health remains native to that representation.

At the split edge, transfer the original physical state into the separately allocated physical-body Actor atomically; remove any competing physical HP/location representation from a disembodied principal. The principal keeps stable build/continuity/knowledge identity and, only where a spell requires it, a closed spell-specific principal LifeStatePolicy for alive/dead disembodied status rather than a duplicate body HP. The relation has exact principal/body/proxy/container/host refs as needed, a profile discriminator, closed phase and required restoration-policy/causal refs. These are declared typed Effect-state members, not an arbitrary `parameters`/`details` graph. Phase legality/reference uniqueness and one current principal-to-active-body relation are checked under the exact profile.

| Value / responsibility | Sole authority after split |
|---|---|
| Stable person identity, build selections, voluntary continuity and knower identity | Principal Actor and existing `world.knowledge.knower_id`; no knowledge union with a host or new body |
| Physical HP, LifeState/death progress and location of each independently affected body | That physical body's native Actor; principal/Effect parameters never store a second writable copy |
| Which principal occupies/controls which body, and spell-specific return/termination obligations | Current typed spell-root Effect relation; source/host policies bind existing invocation/control authorization without creating PLAYER ownership |
| Casting/check statistics from mind versus body, allowed senses/actions and original feature exceptions | Disposable MechanicalContext projection from exact principal/body roles and spell profile; no copied mutable sheet |
| Container/component/item state and actual held/worn placement | Native Asset; split/return transfers exact physical placement with the body, without changing independent legal ownership fiction |
| Pending actions, initiative/turn entitlement, child work and return/death execution | Existing Procedure/Resolution/Continuation owners using the profile's principal/body binding; no free inserted turn or silent reroll |

Magic Jar separates the caster's unattended body, container, caster principal, host body and displaced target principal only when required. The host's physical health/location remain its body authority; the controlling principal provides the exact mental/build projection the spell retains. The target principal's perception from the container is separately eligible. Body death, range, destroyed container and dispel select the precise return/death outcomes from the profile; a body death is not automatically death of every linked principal. On lawful return, merge the chosen body's **current** health/placement back to the stable principal's embodied Actor atomically and retire the no-longer-independent representation through native retirement/retention. Never restore an old HP or inventory snapshot.

Clone owns the immature/mature body and readiness/death/consent relation; at the actual accepted transfer the same principal moves to the eligible body, and the old corpse remains a separately identified native representation with the rule's revival exclusion. Astral Projection binds principal, inert original body, astral proxy and tether/return rules; each independently affected physical representation has its own native health/location. A proxy introduces no new mind or full-PC identity, and ending/return reconciles exact gear/health/life consequences, not a snapshot rollback.

Astral possessions are explicit replicas where the published mode requires them: original native Assets stay with the original body; independently mutable replicas have distinct native Asset IDs with exact spell-root lineage/lifetime and their own current integrity/resources. They are not writable copies of original Asset state. Leaving the plane/returning applies the rule's actual-body/original-possession transfer and replica teardown under the same native relation profile. Clone and other body transfers do not implicitly duplicate gear. The profile preserves stable Effect/application IDs and target references through an explicit typed principal/body subject-binding map; it neither silently retargets historical applications nor resolves old accepted bindings against a new ambient body. An actual permitted target/reference change is a separately validated native transition with causal evidence and currentness rebind. Every Effect's physical/mind/continuity applicability and termination remains its exact rule-defined profile, not one assumed blanket propagation policy.

Every admission profile closes source-defined statistic retention, physical-versus-principal target eligibility, condition/Effect ownership, caster origin, inventory placement, current Procedure bindings, termination and competing episode arbitration. Exact profile semantics can differ; the factoring itself is selected here and cannot be deferred to recipe implementation. Sparse split state exists only during an actual spell obligation. Recovery restores the native relation, principal and all protected bodies/Assets/accepted work, validates the exclusive relation and current revisions, then rebuilds mechanical/knowledge/temporal projections. Required LIVE transfer/freeze routes are the existing native ones; a body on another authority cannot be patched as a local alias. Current one-body Actor/Health/Information contracts need the explicit coordinated SP22 amendment before machine activation.

## 13. Information, illusion and control — SP-11

Objective truth, Actor proposition stance, human disclosure and accepted message are distinct authorities. Charm, compulsion and domination supply exact action restrictions/forced-control effects and resistance/breaking contracts. Existing voluntary PC agency remains player-owned; a mechanically required involuntary action is authorized only by an accepted spell control profile and still does not invent voluntary consent, goals or beliefs. Public NPC cognition is not a substitute for a spell's actual save and control mechanics.

## 14. Counters, defenses and interactions — SP-12

Registered timing windows identify eligible reactors, candidate reactions, fixed trigger evidence, permitted interrupt point and completion/expiry policy. Shield is resolved at its rule-defined triggering attack frontier before final hit/damage acceptance; no retrospective edit of a previously accepted attack is necessary. Counterspell, reaction casting and Counterspell-on-Counterspell must preserve finite typed child/chain bounds and exact costs.

Dispel filters by exact effect/source/spell level and includes the declared per-effect automatic/check rules; it does not terminate every effect on an Actor. Immunity, resistance, vulnerability, prevention and bypass apply in the exact damage policy order with integer rounding and provenance. Same-named spell nonstacking, condition stacking, limited trigger frequency and simultaneous rules choices are independently closed. Order of catalog rows or contributors never determines a mechanical winner by accident.

Antimagic/Globe/Mind Blank and other exception defenses need typed origin/level/magic/source/eligibility facts plus casting, target, effect, item, sensory and travel admission gates. Suppression does not erase permanent nonspell truth or stop time. Exceptions for protected origins and effect-specific dispel immunity remain local recipe data, not a globally assumed immunity to all magic.

## 15. Exceptional composition and time — SP-13

## 18. Large affected sets without changing rules — SP-16

Semantic target count and local processing chunk width differ. A content or configuration limit cannot silently reduce a published spell's affected set, skip creatures or truncate a summon. Source-roster completeness and selector/trigger enrollment are proved before accepting effects. Independent calculations may process complete finite lists in chunks only with proved ordering independence; effect/cost/owner atomicity remains unchanged.

An inseparable closure is staged completely and committed at its lawful native edge. The support profile states tested practical capacity and target conditions truthfully. Long bulk operations may take proportional local work; no promise of constant latency or one universal LIVE commit is made. A complete-support release must test its advertised operating profile against its semantic bounds, including large areas and many due effects.

## 19. Failures and local rule gaps — SP-17

Use the existing typed failure/receipt families for invalid request, unavailable ability, missing declared input, incompatible rules context, currentness/idempotency conflict, capacity and access/durability faults. Exact new reasons require catalog admission under those owners; architecture labels are not new runtime IDs. A genuine player choice, a missing scene fact and a damaged rule package are different outcomes.

Ordinary play never uses web lookup as a missing-capability fallback.

If the player explicitly requests official-source research, the existing policy allows that separate bounded task. It is not a runtime dependency and does not silently alter an already accepted spell outcome or adopted package.
