## Spell grant and recipient-local state

At completion of the **10-minute**, verbal-only casting, choose **up to five creatures** that remained within **30 feet for the entire casting**. Each eligible creature receives Short Rest benefits **and** healing `2d8 + (slot_level−2)d8` for a higher slot. There is no source Wisdom/Charisma/proficiency addend, fixed total, or generic quantity read from sourceActor. No class/type, conscious, willing, or positive-HP target restriction is stated in this body. Actual healing prohibitions/current maximum/LifeState remain health dependencies; the absence of an ordinary-rest start predicate is not a resurrection grant.

The ordinary Short Rest's one-hour elapsed downtime, allowed activities, **at least1 HP to START**, and interruption predicates describe taking that rest. They are not imported as additional conditions on a spell that grants benefits. No actual one-hour rest, global shortened-rest rule, interruption reset, Long Rest benefit, refreshed Hit Dice, or all-resource refill is manufactured.

Each affected target consumes its own Prayer eligibility gate; another caster, slot level, party rest or unrelated target Long Rest cannot bypass/reset it. Only that target's accepted Long Rest resets it. No recovery choice is required to consume this spell's target gate; declining Hit Dice or optional feature recovery does not mean the creature was unaffected. Source does not prescribe a serialization between optional HD choices and direct spell healing; report combined health/resource obligations without inventing ordering. Native simultaneous closure remains FUTURE_PROOF.

**Hit Dice:** the actual recipient may spend zero, one, or more native available dice, choosing another after each roll. Each die heals `max(1, die_roll + recipient Constitution modifier)` before applicable source modifiers and current HP cap. It spends that die; it does not refill spent dice. Mixed class dice remain distinct (`L2082`); native monster dice come from the actual stat block, not caster level/proficiency or a synthesized size-based pool. L17215 expressly notes that some magically created creatures lack dice: do not invent them. Otherworldly Steed L11659 supplies its own d10 count equal to that steed spell's level, not Prayer's slot. Ordinary Long Rest's spent-dice restoration L16865 is excluded.
