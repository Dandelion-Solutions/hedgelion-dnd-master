# R2.5 Evidence Addendum — Agency-Safe Progression and Multiplayer Dramaturg Coordination

Status: **RESEARCH EVIDENCE / PRE-CANDIDATE SYNTHESIS**

Date: 2026-08-24

This addendum extends the R2.5 evidence ledger after owner clarification exposed two material requirements not fully represented in the first Decision Brief:

1. asynchronous play may need to stop at a safe fictional frontier so one player's earlier ChatGPT request cannot erase another player's still-material decision opportunity merely by transport order;
2. independent player chats need shared noncanonical Dramaturg coordination so local preparation remains part of one coherent campaign without prewriting the story.

It does not authorize implementation.

---

## 1. Existing owners and insufficiency boundary

### E1 — LIVE solves shared mutable scene currentness, not campaign-wide dramaturg coordination

`GAME/CORE/LIVE_SCENE.md` activates one live epoch when differently controlled PCs share one concurrently addressable actionable scene. Independent scenes remain separate and use campaign/currentness/chronology routing.

Therefore LIVE prevents divergent mutable truth for one shared scene, but it is not a general cross-scene preparation or campaign-direction channel.

**Disposition:** inherited for factual currentness; insufficient for the newly proven planning consumer.

### E2 — Chronology solves causal ordering, not planning coherence

Step 5.9 and `CHRONOLOGY.md` allow independent scene frontiers and reconcile only when a material causal/temporal bridge appears. Git/host order is not fictional order.

This is sufficient to stop commit arrival order from becoming causality, but chronology does not decide whether an absent player's still-open agency opportunity should be preserved or whether two Dramaturg phases are preparing compatible futures.

**Disposition:** inherited constraint; R2.5 adds collaboration/planning semantics, not a chronology rewrite.

### E3 — Step 4 already defines noncanonical Dramaturg preparation

The Step-4 Dramaturg output is a `PreparationDraft` containing pressures/problems, actors/goals, likely reactions under conditions, possible manifestations, clue/evidence routes, opportunities/constraints, near-horizon developments, assumptions and invalidation/expiry cues.

Prepared scenes/events have no entitlement to occur. Only concrete state established through normal owners may become canon.

**Disposition:** direct semantic base for the proposed Dramaturg horizons.

### E4 — Step 4 already admits cross-player objective developments to Dramaturg

Dramaturg may receive relevant objective truth, hidden facts, threads/processes/deadlines, NPC/faction goals, subject knowledge summaries, cross-player objective developments, player interests/exposure and retained noncanonical preparation.

Therefore the information-eligibility model can already support coordination between player chats. The missing piece is retained, inspectable multiplayer planning continuity, not a new privileged role.

### E5 — R2.3 provides lazy loading and bounded projection

Context Runtime already requires bounded discovery, typed packet closure and selective load. A shared planning artifact need not be preloaded into every Dramaturg phase.

**Disposition:** any shared/local planning horizon must be discoverable/loaded only when materially relevant.

## 2. Agency-safe progression requirement

### A1 — Transport order must not erase a still-material player decision

If resolving player A now would materially remove, narrow or predetermine a decision that player B should still be able to make under fiction/rules, R2.5 needs a coordination barrier before that irreversible resolution.

This is not a global pause and not protection from all world consequences. It protects only an actual still-open human-controlled agency opportunity.

### A2 — Maximal safe frontier

The preferred boundary is the latest state that can be established without consuming the missing player's decision.

Conceptually:

```text
accepted input A
    -> resolve every consequence that is independent of B's still-open choice
    -> STOP at maximal safe frontier
    -> wait/collect B only for the dependent remainder
```

This avoids both extremes:

- resolving too far and stealing B's decision opportunity;
- freezing the whole scene/campaign earlier than necessary.

### A3 — Agency dependency is scope-local

A waiting dependency blocks only the bounded scope whose resolution depends on the missing contribution. Independent scenes/processes continue under their native owners.

### A4 — No silence-as-consent and no offline immunity

Absence/latency never authorizes PC speech/action/consent.

Conversely, a PC is not protected from automatic world/rules consequences merely because the human is absent when the PC has no genuine decision/reaction opportunity under the applicable owner contract.

### A5 — External human coordination is allowed but not authority

Players may coordinate through any external channel. A statement in player A's chat that player B agreed to a voluntary action is a coordination hint, not authority over B's PC.

Each controlled PC's voluntary contribution must be accepted from its authorized player/session unless an explicit separate control-transfer contract exists.

## 3. Two-level Dramaturg coordination requirement

### D1 — Player-local Dramaturg horizon

Each player/chat may retain bounded noncanonical near-horizon preparation relevant to that player's current scene/trajectory.

Local horizons may differ substantially in tone, pressures, NPC focus and likely near-term developments while remaining compatible with shared canon and applicable common planning constraints.

### D2 — Shared Dramaturg horizon exists only in multiplayer

A multiplayer-only shared noncanonical planning projection is justified because multiple independent Dramaturg phases need a common campaign-level preparation basis.

Singleplayer does not need a second shared level merely for symmetry.

### D3 — Shared horizon is coordination, not plot authority

The shared horizon may retain campaign-level pressures, important cross-player developments, active common threads, convergence possibilities, major mystery/revelation constraints, common tone/premise constraints, assumptions and invalidation cues.

It must distinguish:

```text
SOURCE-ANCHORED CONSTRAINT
    references accepted canonical/current owners

PROVISIONAL DRAMATURGIC DIRECTION
    retained planning possibility only
```

Neither type gives the horizon independent factual authority. The first derives its constraint from referenced owners; the second remains provisional.

### D4 — Preparation never has entitlement to occur

New accepted player decisions, Actor decisions, mechanics, causal developments or owner state may invalidate any local/shared preparation.

Canonical rule:

> **CANON INVALIDATES PREPARATION; PREPARATION DOES NOT CONSTRAIN CANONICAL PLAYER/ACTOR FREEDOM.**

No replacement twist, hidden duplicate actor/object, coincidence or forced redirection may be created solely to restore an invalidated prepared trajectory.

### D5 — Shared coherence constrains preparation, not player freedom

Local horizons should be compatible with the current applicable shared planning basis so independent Masters do not silently develop incompatible campaign ontologies/directions.

This is a planning-consistency relation, not a rule that players must follow the common horizon.

### D6 — Lazy relevance and rebasing

Shared/local horizons are not mandatory per-turn preload.

A Dramaturg phase may use compact discovery metadata to determine whether a newer shared generation materially affects its task. If relevant, the local horizon is revalidated/rebased against current canon and the current shared planning basis; if irrelevant, the full shared artifact need not be loaded.

No background rewrite of every player's preparation is required when one chat advances.

Previous disposition: **CONDITIONAL / DORMANT**.

Preserved trigger: activate when retained separate planning state is proven necessary beyond ordinary ephemeral/provisional `PreparationDraft`.

R2.5 multiplayer now supplies that concrete consumer:

> several independent ChatGPT Dramaturg phases need retained shared planning continuity so local preparation remains coherent across host gaps and player-local scenes.

**New R2.5 disposition:** **ACTIVATED NARROWLY**.

Scope of activation:

- player-local Dramaturg horizon;
- multiplayer-only shared Dramaturg horizon;
- noncanonical, inspectable, source-bounded;
- no global plot database, campaign director, scheduler, authored arc authority or mandatory standalone Narrative Dynamics stage.

## 5. Updated synthesis

The first R2.5 recommendation is refined to:

> **B3 — AGENCY-SAFE SCOPED COLLABORATION + TWO-LEVEL DRAMATURG COORDINATION**

Its orthogonal responsibilities are:

```text
LIVE/current owners
    shared factual mutable-scene consistency

CHRONOLOGY
    causal/order consistency across independent scopes

R2.5 collaboration
    preserve still-open human agency and collect bounded joint input

R2.5 Dramaturg horizons
    preserve noncanonical planning coherence across independent Masters
```

No one layer substitutes for another.
