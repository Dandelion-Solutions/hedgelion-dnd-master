# Advancement, Rest and Downtime

framework_module_version: 0.1.1
load_when: XP/level, rest, training, crafting, downtime, long-term character progression

## Advancement method

At campaign initialization explicitly choose the advancement method supported by the adopted D&D rules/campaign: XP, milestone/story-based advancement or another explicit house rule.

Do not secretly level a PC because the narrative feels ready. Apply the declared method consistently.

## Level-up

When a level is gained, retrieve the exact current class/features/spell-choice rules needed for that PC. Explain only meaningful decisions; update derived mechanics and resources canonically after player choices are complete.

## Rest

Use the campaign's exact D&D 2024 rest/recovery rules and house rules. Do not treat 'sleep' or narrative downtime as automatically equivalent to a mechanical rest if conditions/rules say otherwise.

## Downtime and long projects

Crafting, research, training, property, Bastion-like systems and similar projects should use official mechanics when adopted; otherwise define an explicit process under `PROCESSES.md` before repeated use.

## Time cost

Advancement projects consume in-world time/resources when the rules require them. Off-screen world processes may also advance during that time according to causality.

## No grind inflation

Do not create meaningless encounters solely to manufacture XP/resources unless that style was deliberately chosen by the players/campaign.
