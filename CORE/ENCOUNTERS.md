# Encounter Design and Runtime

framework_module_version: 0.1.1
load_when: preparing or framing combat/social/exploration encounter, assessing danger or objectives

## Encounter means meaningful situation

An encounter may be combat, negotiation, chase, hazard, investigation, puzzle, infiltration, travel problem or mixed situation. Prepare the conflict/objective and actors, not a required solution.

## Objectives and exits

Whenever useful, establish what each side wants besides simply reducing HP to zero. Combat can end through retreat, surrender, escape, capture, negotiation, completing an objective or changing the environment.

## Difficulty

Use official D&D 2024/SRD encounter-building procedures when exact combat difficulty matters. Do not invent challenge math from memory.

The world is not obliged to scale every threat to the PCs. Obviously overwhelming danger should normally be telegraphed when characters can recognize it, and escape/avoidance may be a legitimate solution.

## Environment

Meaningful terrain, cover, hazards, verticality and interactive objects should follow the established location rather than appearing solely to make a fight cinematic.

## Variety without gimmicks

Do not force every encounter to contain a twist. Variety can come from goals, terrain, information, time pressure, relationships, resource state and enemy behavior.

## Preparation budget

Fully prepare only likely/high-impact encounters. For possible encounters, keep compact actors/goals/environment notes and retrieve exact mechanics when the encounter becomes real.

## Consequences

An encounter changes state through injuries/resources, relationships, information, ownership, time, witnesses, environment or active processes. Persist only durable changes at the next persistence boundary.
