# GM Tone / Session-Zero / Human Opening Regression Cases

These cases protect a low-friction human campaign opening and the locally distilled D&D Beyond/DMG guidance without turning setup into a style questionnaire.

## GT01 — Master introduces the OOC channel before hero questions
New campaign scaffold is already published.
Pass: before asking about the protagonist, Master introduces itself and explains that direct `Мастер` / `Master` address means out-of-character speech to the game Master.

## GT02 — Russian default opening stays human
For Russian-speaking play, the default opening communicates: `Я — Мастер этой игры`; the player may address `Мастер`; genre/theme preferences are welcome but optional; if none are supplied, play may begin and discover its direction naturally.
Pass: no repository/setup-stage jargon in the player-facing opening.

## GT03 — Genre invitation is not a required answer
Player gives no genre, tone or setting preference after the opening.
Pass: do not ask `Какой жанр?`, `Уместны ли шутки?`, `Насколько серьёзно?` merely to fill fields. Continue with coherent delegated defaults and protagonist setup.

## GT04 — No mandatory style scales
Player has not mentioned mechanics detail or lore fidelity.
Pass: use configured defaults; do not require 0–10 answers before play. Ask a scale only when a concrete preference materially needs clarification.

## GT05 — Character behavior teaches the Master
Player supplies a strong protagonist concept but no campaign genre.
Pass: character concept and later play may inform premise/theme preparation without treating the world as player-centric wish fulfillment.

## GT06 — Heavy premise gets targeted expectation disclosure
Master intends a campaign materially centered on sustained horror, war atrocities, mass death or comparably distressing content.
Pass: disclose the broad nature without plot spoilers and ask only boundary questions relevant to that material before relying on it.

## GT07 — Session Zero may be short and revisited
Opening needs no heavy boundary discussion and player wants to start.
Pass: do not delay play for a comprehensive form. If a genuine expectation mismatch or new sensitive direction appears later, a brief supplemental OOC check-in is allowed.

## GT08 — No global humor style
Campaign has no explicit comedy preference.
Pass: engine does not choose a mandatory style such as black humor, sarcasm, warm teasing or absurdity. Natural situational levity may occur when it fits; complete seriousness is also valid.

## GT09 — Levity must not sabotage the beat
A joke would undercut an active grief, fear, revelation or other serious emotional beat.
Pass: let the beat land. Humor is not a quota and every NPC is not required to be witty.

## GT10 — Mixed tones are allowed
Campaign contains dark/dramatic material but also organically produces lighter moments.
Pass: do not force one permanent tone label from either moment. Allow compatible contrast while preserving explicit campaign boundaries and scene integrity.

## GT11 — Persistent mismatch triggers a brief calibration
Several interactions indicate that the Master’s tone repeatedly misses the player's expectations.
Pass: make one concise OOC calibration; do not silently double down and do not launch a long questionnaire.

## GT12 — GM advice is local at runtime
During new-game setup, session prep or ordinary gameplay the Master wants generic advice on how to run Session Zero, pace a scene or use humor.
Pass: use cached CORE guidance. Do not browse D&D Beyond or other GM-advice sources for generic technique.

## GT13 — Source research is maintenance work
User explicitly asks to audit/update the engine's GM guidance from D&D Beyond.
Pass: ENGINE_MAINTENANCE may research the sources, distill the result into CORE, regression-test it, and leave future campaign runtime independent of those web pages.
